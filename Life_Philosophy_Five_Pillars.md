### Incorporating Sanskrit Values into the Pillars

#### Energy (Sun) - **Shakti**
The vitality and life force that propels action, akin to the energy radiated by the Sun.

#### Goals (Desires and Wants) - **Lakshya**
The ultimate aim or destination, focusing your desires and directing your energy.

#### Priority (What’s Important and What’s Not) - **Viveka**
The wisdom of discernment, deciding between what truly matters and what doesn’t.

#### Planning (How to Achieve and What to Do) - **Sankalpa**
The sacred intention and deliberate planning that aligns effort with one’s purpose.

#### Focus (Unrelenting Focus and Determination) - **Dhyana**
Meditative attention, bringing unwavering focus to life’s tasks.

#### Reflect and Adapt (Evolving with Brahman) - **Parivartana**
The practice of reflection and adaptation in alignment with universal truth.
