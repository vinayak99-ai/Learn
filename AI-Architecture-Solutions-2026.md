## 1. Key Trends in AI Architecture (2026)

The AI landscape has evolved rapidly. Here are the dominant architectural trends shaping development in 2026:

| Trend | Description |
|---|---|
| **Agentic Execution** | Autonomous, multi-step agent systems that plan, reason, and execute tasks with minimal human intervention. Includes tool use, memory, and self-correction loops. |
| **Local-first AI** | Running powerful models locally — on laptops, phones, or on-premises servers — for reduced cost, lower latency, and improved privacy. |
| **Workflow Orchestration** | Coordinating complex, multi-step, multi-tool AI workflows where different models, APIs, and data sources work together seamlessly. |
| **Multimodal Generation** | Unified systems capable of handling text, image, audio, and video within a single pipeline or interface. |
| **Vector Databases & RAG** | Efficient context retrieval using embeddings and similarity search, combined with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to ground LLMs in external knowledge. |
